# PocketDeploy project

React 19 + Vite + Tailwind CSS v4. See `project.md` for the structure, rules and routes.

- Entry: `src/main.jsx` → `src/App.jsx` (routes) → `src/pages/`
- Custom CSS and keyframes: `src/App.css`
- Animations: `src/components/motion.jsx` (`Reveal`, `SplitText`)
- Dark mode: `src/hooks/useTheme.jsx`






Aapki website ka naam hai All Businesses — India’s B2B Marketplace for Verified Suppliers.

Short mein, ye ek B2B business marketplace website hai, jahan businesses ek-doosre se connect kar sakte hain.

Ye website kya kaam karti hai?

Buyers ke liye: Apni requirement ke according products ya suppliers dhoondhna.

Suppliers ke liye: Apne products aur business ko online showcase karna.

Business connections: Buyers aur sellers ko ek platform par lana.

Business growth: Suppliers ko naye customers aur business enquiries paane mein madad karna.

Ye kaise kaam karti hai?

Buyer product ya supplier search karta hai

Relevant suppliers ki details dekhta hai

Buyer aur supplier contact karke business deal karte hain

Ek example samjho

Maan lo, Delhi ka ek retailer bulk mein cosmetics khareedna chahta hai. Woh is platform par cosmetics suppliers search kar sakta hai aur suitable supplier se contact karke rates aur quantity discuss kar sakta hai.

Ye IndiaMART jaisi website ka concept hai, lekin aapki website ke actual features ko poori tarah verify nahi kar paaya, kyunki diye gaye link ko access karne par Vercel login page redirect ho raha tha.

Isliye ye bhi confirm karna zaroori hai ki supplier verification, registration, product listing aur enquiry forms sach mein kaam karte hain ya abhi sirf design ka hissa hain.
